Being a landlord looked good on paper. Then came the late rent, the furnace call at 2 a.m. in January, the turnover repairs and the tenant who stopped answering the phone. If you're ready to be done, you don't have to wait for the lease to end, or for the tenant to leave, to sell.

## Signs it may be time to sell

- Rent comes in late, or not at all, more often than it comes in on time.
- Repairs, turnovers and rising condo fees are eating the cash flow.
- The property needs big-ticket items (a roof, furnace, windows or sewer line) you don't want to fund.
- You're dealing with a tenancy dispute, bylaw complaints or an unresponsive property manager.
- Your mortgage renews soon at a higher rate.
- You'd rather have the equity working somewhere else, or not working at all.

## Your options as a landlord

- **Hire a property manager.** Hands off the day-to-day, but it typically costs a percentage of the rent plus leasing fees, and the repairs are still yours.
- **Wait for the lease to end and sell it vacant.** You'll likely need turnover repairs and months of carrying costs with no rent coming in. For a rental in good shape, listing it vacant may still net the most; I'll tell you if I think so.
- **Sell it occupied to an investor.** That's what I do.

## Selling with tenants in place

The tenancy stays in effect when a rental is sold, and the new owner steps into your shoes as landlord. Here's how it works:

- **One visit, on the tenants' schedule,** with the written notice Alberta's rules require for entry.
- **Rent, deposits and the lease handled at closing** through the lawyers' statement of adjustments.
- **After closing,** the tenants deal with the new owner, not you.

## What if the tenant isn't paying?

I can buy with a non-paying tenant, and even with a dispute already underway at the Residential Tenancy Dispute Resolution Service or in court. Keep following the legal process until closing; lockouts and utility shutoffs can create liability for landlords. After closing, the tenancy is the new owner's to manage.
