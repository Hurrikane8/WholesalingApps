Edmonton has thousands of condo townhouses, many of them in complexes built in the 1970s and '80s. They made great starter homes. But as those complexes age, owners are facing rising fees, big repair bills for roofs, siding and windows, higher insurance costs and, sometimes, special assessments that arrive with little warning.

If that's where you are, you're not stuck. We buy condo townhouses and apartment condos as-is, for cash, on the closing date you choose.

## Why condos can be hard to sell right now

- **High or rising condo fees** shrink the pool of buyers, because lenders count the fees when they decide how much a buyer can borrow.
- **Special assessments** scare off buyers and can derail deals when they come up in the condo documents.
- **Thin reserve funds** and big repairs on the horizon make buyers and lenders nervous.
- **Condo document conditions** give buyers a way out after reading the minutes and financials, sometimes weeks into a deal.
- **Rental and pet restrictions** can rule out whole groups of buyers.

## When selling to a cash buyer makes sense

A direct sale is often a good fit when:

- A special assessment has been announced or is being discussed
- Your fees have gone up faster than you can comfortably afford
- The unit needs updating that you'd rather not pay for
- You've had a deal collapse over the condo documents
- You're a landlord ready to sell a unit with a tenant in place
- You need to move quickly for work, family or finances

## How we buy condos

- **We read the documents so you don't have to worry about them.** We factor the fees, reserve fund and upcoming work into a firm offer instead of using them as a reason to walk away.
- **As-is condition.** No painting, flooring or updates first.
- **Tenants welcome.** We can buy with the tenancy in place.
- **Arrears and assessments handled at closing.** Unpaid fees or an assessment can be paid from the sale proceeds, and the contract will spell out exactly who pays what.
