This Privacy Policy explains how {{legalName}} ("{{company}}," "we," "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit {{siteUrl}} or contact us about selling a property.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you request an offer, call, text or email us, we collect what you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, the property address, and details about the property and your situation.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we receive basic technical information such as your browser type, device, pages visited, the page that referred you and approximate location derived from your IP address. We may use cookies and similar technologies for this purpose (see below).

**Advertising and campaign information.** If you arrive from an ad or a tracked link, we may record campaign parameters (for example, UTM tags) so we know which marketing is working.

## How we use your information

- To respond to your inquiry, evaluate your property and prepare an offer
- To communicate with you by phone, email and, if you consent, text message
- To complete a purchase you've agreed to, working with title companies, closing attorneys and other parties to the transaction
- To improve our website and marketing
- To comply with the law and protect our rights

## Text messaging (SMS)

If you check the box on our form or otherwise opt in, we may send you text messages about your property inquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out at any time, or **HELP** for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of receiving an offer or selling your property.

**No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.** Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver messages.

## How we share information

We do **not** sell your personal information. We share it only:

- With service providers who help us run our business (for example, our CRM, email, phone and hosting providers), who may use it only to provide services to us
- With parties involved in a transaction you have agreed to, such as a title company, closing attorney, lender or, if we assign our purchase contract, the buyer to whom it is assigned
- When required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our business, our customers or others

## Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and analytics tools, such as Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors use our site and to measure advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

## How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to you, complete any transaction, and meet our legal, tax and record-keeping obligations.

## Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

## Children's privacy
