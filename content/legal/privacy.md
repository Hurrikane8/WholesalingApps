This Privacy Policy explains how {{legalName}} ("{{company}}," "we," "us") collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information when you visit {{siteUrl}} or contact us about selling a property or joining our investor buyers list. We follow applicable Canadian privacy laws, including Alberta's *Personal Information Protection Act*.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you request an offer, join our buyers list, call, text or email us, we collect what you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, the property address, details about the property and your situation, and, for investors, your buying criteria.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we receive basic technical information such as your browser type, device, pages visited, the page that referred you and approximate location derived from your IP address. We may use cookies and similar technologies for this purpose (see below).

**Advertising and campaign information.** If you arrive from an ad or a tracked link, we may record campaign parameters (for example, UTM tags) so we know which marketing is working.

## How we use your information

- To respond to your inquiry, evaluate your property and prepare an offer
- To communicate with you by phone, email and, where you've consented, text message
- For investors on our buyers list: to send you properties and updates that match your criteria
- To complete a transaction you've agreed to, working with lawyers and other parties to the transaction
- To improve our website and marketing
- To comply with the law and protect our rights

## Consent

By submitting a form or contacting us, you consent to us using your information for the purposes described above. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see "Your choices"), subject to legal or contractual restrictions.

## Text messages and email

**Sellers:** if you tick the box on our offer form, we may text you about your property inquiry. Consent is not required to get an offer or sell your property.

**Investors:** if you join our buyers list, you agree to receive property deals and investor updates from us by email and text message.

Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying **STOP** to a text or clicking "unsubscribe" in an email, and we'll stop within the time the law requires. Reply **HELP** for help.

**No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.** Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver messages.

## How we share information

We do **not** sell your personal information. We share it only:

- With service providers who help us run our business (for example, our CRM, email, phone and website hosting providers), who may use it only to provide services to us. Some of these providers may store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under the laws of that country.
- With parties involved in a transaction you have agreed to, such as lawyers, lenders or, if we assign our purchase contract, the buyer to whom it is assigned
- When required or permitted by law

## Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and analytics tools, such as Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors use our site and to measure advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

## How long we keep information

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to you, complete any transaction, and meet our legal, tax and record-keeping obligations, including keeping records of the consent you gave us.

## Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, withdraw your consent, or ask us to stop contacting you by calling or texting {{phone}}. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.
