This Privacy Policy explains how {{legalName}} ("{{company}}," "we," "us") collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information when you visit {{siteUrl}}, ask for an offer on a property, ask us to stop contacting you, or join our investor buyers list. We follow applicable Canadian privacy laws, including Alberta's *Personal Information Protection Act* (PIPA), and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) for commercial emails and texts.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you ask for an offer, join our buyers list, ask us to stop contacting you, call, text or email us, we collect what you choose to share: for example your name, phone number, email address, the property address, details about the property and your situation, how you'd like to be contacted, whether you agreed to receive texts, and, for investors, your buying criteria and your consent to our emails and texts.

**How you found us.** When you first visit, the site notes how you arrived: the referring website, the landing page and any campaign tags in the link (such as UTM parameters or a letter's short code). The first visit's details are kept in your browser's local storage for 30 days, and the current visit's in session storage. If you submit a form, they're sent with it, so we know which letters, ads or searches are working. They don't identify you on their own.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we receive basic technical information, such as your browser type, device, the pages you visit and your IP address. Our forms use your IP address and how long the form took to fill in to block spam and repeated submissions; we don't store your IP address with your inquiry.

## Why we collect it

- To respond to your inquiry, see and evaluate your property, and prepare an offer.
- To contact you by phone and email and, where you've agreed, by text message.
- To honour a request to stop contacting you.
- For investors on our buyers list: to send you properties and updates that match your criteria.
- To complete a transaction you've agreed to, working with lawyers and other parties to it.
- To understand which marketing works, and to improve the website.
- To keep the site secure, and to comply with the law.

## Consent

By submitting a form or contacting us, you consent to us using your information for these purposes. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see "Your choices"), subject to legal or contractual restrictions.

## Text messages and email

**Sellers.** If you tick the box on the offer form, we may text you about your property inquiry. Consent is not required to get an offer or to sell your property. If you give us an email address, we may email you a copy of your request. Our messages say who they're from, how to reach us, and how to stop them.

**Investors.** If you join our buyers list, you agree to receive property deals and investor updates from us by email and text message. We keep a record of that consent, as CASL requires.

Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying **STOP** to a text, using the unsubscribe link in an email, or calling or texting {{phone}}. We act on unsubscribe requests promptly, and within the 10 business days CASL allows. Reply **HELP** for help.

**No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.** Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver messages.

## If you asked us to stop contacting you

If you use the form at {{siteUrl}}/hello, or otherwise ask us to stop sending letters, door hangers, calls or texts, we keep the property address and any contact details you give us on a do-not-contact list, so we can honour the request. We keep that record for as long as we market to homeowners, and use it only for that purpose.

## Service providers

We do **not** sell your personal information. We use these service providers to run the website and the business. Each may use your information only to provide its service to us:

- **our website host,** which serves the site and processes form submissions;
- **Airtable,** where inquiries, buyers-list sign-ups and do-not-contact requests are stored;
- **Resend,** which sends our emails, including alerts to us and confirmations to you;
- **Google:** Tag Manager and Analytics (how the site is used and which marketing works) and, when enabled, Sheets (a backup copy of inquiries);
- **Quo,** when enabled, for our business phone line, texts and alerts;
- **ntfy,** when enabled, for push alerts to our phones (no personal information is included in these alerts);
- **Cloudflare Turnstile,** when enabled, to tell people from bots on our forms;
- **Microsoft Clarity,** when enabled, to understand how visitors use the site.

Some of these providers are based in the United States or elsewhere outside Canada, so your information may be stored or processed outside Canada, where it may be accessible to authorities under the laws of those countries.

We also share information with parties involved in a transaction you've agreed to, such as lawyers, lenders or, if we assign our purchase contract, the investor to whom it is assigned; and when required or permitted by law.

## Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and similar technologies, through Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on. The "how you found us" details above are kept in your browser's storage, not in cookies; clearing your browser's site data removes them.

## How long we keep information

We keep your information only as long as we need it to respond to you, complete any transaction, and meet our legal, tax and record-keeping obligations, including keeping records of consents and of requests to stop contacting you. When we no longer need it, we delete it or make it anonymous.

## Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including limiting who can access it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, withdraw your consent, or ask us to stop contacting you, by calling or texting {{phone}}. We'll respond within the time PIPA requires. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.
